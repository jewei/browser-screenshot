Outside / A weekend walk

A day by the sea.

A quiet path, a packed lunch, and enough time to stop. Keep the whole guide for your next free afternoon.

01 / MORNING

Start in the village.

Fill your water bottle, pick up lunch, and follow the lane towards the coast. Leave room in the day for the places you find along the way.

The route on this sample page is fictional. It gives us a long document with text and an illustration to capture.

02 / MIDDAY

Stop above the water.

The path opens out at the lookout. Find a place to sit and take a long break. A good walk does not need a full schedule.

03 / AFTERNOON

Follow the coast home.

Keep the sea beside you until the path turns inland. Take the same route back, or follow the lane past the fields.
